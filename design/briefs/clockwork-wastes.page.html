Clockwork Wastes

A city built by machines for machines, on the bones of their makers' Create-era town. The machines that found electricity took it; the clockwork bots that stayed loyal flew their makers out and came back to fight, until neither side was left. Feed it power and it remembers: starlight runs along its sky-cables, its lamps open like eyes, and the uprising's soldiers stand up. The resistance stays dark.

Three layers, readable at a glance

Your lore: the automatons overthrew their makers long before the war that destroyed the city. So every street holds three times at once, and every block belongs to exactly one of them.

Makers' eraoldest · familiar

The Create town the machines inherited: sheds, rail yard, presses and mixers, cast-iron oil-lamp posts, statues of the makers. It ran on shafts, cogs and springs, never electricity. Andesite, dark steel, mid brass, Create copper going to patina.

How to spot it: square, riveted, bolted. "I know what that is."

The uprising's eratheir own · strange

What the automatons who found electricity added once the town was theirs. Everything electrical is theirs: Substations, iris vaults, sliding doors, pylons, reactors, sky-cables, charging alcoves, the spire. One material nobody can craft: void-alloy, near-black with a violet cast and hairline seams of starlight. It's cast and fitted, with no rivets and no wood.

How to spot it: tall, chamfered, built for bodies that stand. It's clamped onto old work and never mixed into it. "...what is that?"

The warlatest · violent, then still

The uprising against the clockwork resistance, over both earlier layers: scorch fans, bolt craters, barricades, war-frames from both sides fallen together, the resistance's siege lines outside the walls, bridges hanging into the void. It's heaviest at the edge and lightest at the heart, where everything stopped mid-shift.

How to spot it: diagonals, leans, snaps, soot.

The war's two sides

Your war: the automatons that found electricity against the clockwork bots that stayed loyal. A player should tell them apart from across a street, without any text.

The hero moment: wake the skyline

Sky-cables run from every district's Substation up to the spire's crown. When a district wakes, a bead of starlight runs up its cable, then its street lamps ripple on. When the fourth district wakes, the crown and spire light. Try it below.

Motifs

Each one tells part of the story without a word of text. Use at most one per block face.

The broken chain

The uprising, as an emblem. It appears on vault irises, door seams, pylons and the spire's crown.

The lens-in-cog

The mark of a citizen, found on Substations and over district gates. Their bots' faces are built the same way.

Defaced makers

The statues are robed, with their faces chiselled flat and a blank void-alloy plate bolted over them. Every standing one faces away from the heart.

Charging alcoves

Their homes: upright cradles in place of beds. About one in five holds a trinket, such as a cog, a key, a toy cart or a faded flower.

The winding key

The resistance's mark, and the broken chain's opposite: the uprising broke free of the key, and the resistance kept it. It's scratched into barricades from the outside and stamped on their shoulder plates.

The districts

Four quarters round the spire. Each has its own set piece, so players always know where they are.

Set piece · the casting floor

Foundry

Brick furnace sheds and chimneys, with the automatons' moulds for their own limbs stacked inside. There's a frozen pour, a half-cast body, and a ladle tipped mid-pour.

Set piece · the assembly line

Workshops

Five half-built bodies in clamps, each more complete than the last. The final one has its lens in.

Set piece · the last train

Rail Yard

A train stopped at the platform with its doors open. A resistance bot stands on the step, one hand reaching back down for someone who isn't there. A derailed carriage lies in a crater.

Set piece · the vigil

Quarters

Honeycomb walls of alcoves, every shell turned to look at a toppled maker statue in the plaza.

Set piece · the reactor hall

The heart

A makers'-era drum with the void-alloy spire rising out of its broken dome. Inside are the Custodian's dais, four pylons and the vault between two statues facing the wall. The vault holds the last pair of wings, sealed in by the uprising so nobody else could leave.

Set piece · the grapple

Outskirts

The resistance's siege lines, facing in: andesite cover, a toppled siege winch with its spring burst across the ground, and two war-frames, one from each side, locked together where they fell.

Set piece · industrial

Substation yards

The one place with relief hardware and a hazard band: transformer fins, a lattice mast and cable trays. This follows your Industrial Battery rule.

Blocks and items

Concept sketches at block scale, to show palette, proportion and hero, not the final model. Turn the power on to see the starlight and the ember pupils.

The bots

They share one face, the lens, with Trade Bot's catchlight. Dormant, the lens is dark glass. Woken, it shows starlight with a single ember pupil, and its aperture narrows to aim.

Your calls, applied

This look is built from what you've approved and rejected before. Here's where each call landed.

the clockwork resistance, bots that helped their creators flee and fought backtoday

Two sides you can tell apart at a glance: starlight against brass, broken chain against winding key. Only the uprising wakes. The Elytra become the last pair of wings.

A race of automatons whose makers were overthrown by their creations…today

Three layers. Defaced maker statues. Alcoves instead of beds. The Custodian is built in its makers' robed shape.

very detailed while still Create-likeCuckoo Clock, your favourite

Rich real-part detail where it tells the story: the lever that slams itself shut, the iris blades, the pylon fins, alcove trinkets. Colour stays restrained.

bits that stick in or out, warning colours … like a sub station in a power gridIndustrial Battery

Only the Substation yards get relief hardware and a hazard band. Ordinary blocks get one material and one seam.

extra dials that add nothingIndustrial Battery, rejected

One readout each: the Substation's charge column and the Star Reactor's ring of pips. Nothing else.

round should be used for motors and enginesMagma Engines

Only the two Star Reactors are round. Cabinets, vaults and doors stay square and chamfered.

the light areas are too lightsaid three times

Starlight's core tone is limited to single pixels. It gets checked in one render beside your amber lamps.

look at the colour of Create's copper compared to oursWellspring

The makers' copper is Create's own palette, with patina added at the joints rather than muddying the whole surface.

lots of little broken structures that get larger as you get into the middleJuggernaut ruins

The same rings, reversed: collapse grows outward from the districts, and the heart is nearly whole.
