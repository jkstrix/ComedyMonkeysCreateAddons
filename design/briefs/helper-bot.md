# Design Brief: Helper Bot

Character brief for Helper Bot, the train driver and shopkeeper robot block: design, motion, rig and preview clips.
Gameplay: [design/specs/helper-bot.md](../specs/helper-bot.md). Family reference: [Trade Bot brief](trade-bot.md), [trade-bot.bbmodel](../models/trade-bot.bbmodel), generated by [trade-bot.gen.js](../models/trade-bot.gen.js).
Model (to build): `design/models/helper-bot.bbmodel`, generated by `design/models/helper-bot.gen.js`.

## Hard Requirements
- "a train driver robot that is part block (doesn't move from the spot) so he can't fall off, a bit like blaze burners when you get them to drive create trains"
- "He should be based on the trade bot's look and feel"
- "the ability to be dyed in a crafting bench etc (one dye per one driver)"
- "relatively cheap as he's just replacing the blaze burner with something more fun and animated"
- Full Trade Bot size: **1.5 blocks tall** (24 px) (spec D13)
- **Ducks under a solid block** to fit within one block: telescoping neck, head lowered (spec D17)
- Anchored on a **swivel post on a foot plate** (user, brief round 1)
- The dye goes on the **body panels and forearms** only; the steel frame, eye pods and brass stay put (user, brief round 1)
- **Train cap** only while driving a train with a schedule; **logistics cap** while keeping shop; the train cap wins (spec D20)
- Eyes glow on the model only (spec D24)
- "winding mechanism should be removed from the back as he doesn't need it": **no wind-up key** (user, 2026-10-27)
- "Lets adjust his face a little bit too, he should look like a variant (minor changes)": a **brass whisker grille** under the eyes and **cool cyan irises** (user, 2026-10-27)
- Reactions from the spec's state table: throttle up, cruising, braking, waiting at a station (checks his clock), departing (toot and wave), honk (pulls the whistle cord), no path (shrug, looks at the map), paused or manual driving (arms folded; taps the glass and points at the schedule), crash (jolt, dizzy, sulk), no schedule (looks at you expectantly), passenger (looks out, waves), off duty (watches players, waves when right-clicked), hostile (eyes wide, shrinks down, never leaves), rain (wipes his lenses), thunder (flinches), shop (nods when an order is placed)

## Asset Type
Character block: an animated block drawn by a block-entity renderer, and on trains by a contraption visual. 1×1 footprint, **24 px tall** (16 ducked). Built like Trade Bot: `modded_entity` format (box UV), code-driven animation from the preview timings (no GeckoLib), one generator script for geometry, texture, rig and clips. The preview texture holds the props; the game texture is generated without them.

## Fantasy
A proud little clockwork engine driver, bolted to his post, who lives for the timetable: pocket watch in his hand, cap on his head, a toot on the whistle and a wave for everyone on the platform.

## Hero Element
**Trade Bot's binocular eye pods, wearing a conductor's cap, over a brass moustache.** The family face is still the hero (two chunky steel pods, brass rims, a catchlight each, sliding lid plates), made a variant by two small changes: **cool cyan irises** (a signal-lamp glow instead of Trade Bot's amber) and a **brass whisker grille** under the bridge, his voice grille shaped like an engine driver's walrus moustache. The cap perches across both pods like a hat on a pair of binoculars. From a distance you read "Trade Bot's cousin, at work".

## Theme
Same robot family, same Create materials, same muted finish. He sits beside Trade Bot, Create's Train Controls and seats, and the mod's dyed Fluid Lines.

## Shape Language
**Circles**, as Trade Bot: round lenses, a round foot plate (stepped), a round post and collar, cogs, the watch, the whistle bell. Squares appear only in the steel frame and the controls he grips.

## Silhouette Description
**A wine glass with binoculars on top, in a cap.** Trade Bot's silhouette with the wheel swapped for a stem and foot: a wide flat round foot (12 px), a thin post rising from a brass collar, a rounded body (10 px wide), a thin neck and the wide 14 px binocular head. The cap sits centred on top and a small whistle pipe pokes up from the left pod where Trade Bot's aerial was. Side view: he leans slightly forward over the post, toward the controls, arms reaching out. Ducked, he's a squat mushroom: body down on the collar, head sunk, cap tipped back.

## Primary Forms
1. **Eye pod pair** (the hero): Trade Bot's pods with **cyan irises**. Two 6×5×5 px pods, 2 px apart, joined by a 2 px bridge, at y17–22; 4×3 lens in a 1 px brass rim; 6×3 steel lid plate in front.
2. **Body**: Trade Bot's 8×9×8 px chassis plus 1 px side panels (10 px wide), at y7–16, stepped corners. The andesite panels are the dye zone. **No drawer and no trade lamp**: a chest pocket and the schedule clip take their place.
3. **Foot and post**: a round andesite foot plate (12 px across, stepped, y0–2) in a steel rim; a brass swivel collar ring (5 px, y2–3) with a small Create cog on its side; a 3×3 steel post (y3–6) that telescopes down into the foot; a short copper gimbal spring (y6–7) the body rides on.

## Secondary Forms
- **Whisker grille**: a brass moustache under the bridge, in front of the lower lenses: a short centre bar on a steel stem from the bridge, and a half on each side drooping to a tip (about 10 px across, y16.25–18), tucked right against the lens rims, just under the lenses (user: "a little closer and a little higher"). Vertical dark slots make it read as a voice grille. Each half twitches on its own.
- **Telescoping neck**: as Trade Bot's (slides up 2 px for surprise, down into the body to duck).
- **Arms**: as Trade Bot's (shoulder cog, steel upper arm, forearm in the dye zone, two-prong brass claw), long enough to rest on the Train Controls' levers in the block in front.
- **Whistle**: a short copper pipe rising from the top of the **left pod** (where Trade Bot's aerial was), ending in a small brass steam-whistle bell at y24. A dark pull cord with a brass toggle bead hangs down the outside of the left pod, so his left claw can reach it. The pipe is springy and lags like the aerial did.
- **Pocket watch**: a brass hunter watch (3×3×1 px, hinged lid) in a pocket on his left chest, on a short brass chain. He takes it out with his left claw.
- **Schedule clip**: a small brass clip on his right chest. While the train has a schedule it holds a paper schedule card (2×3 px).

## Interesting Features
- **The caps.** The train cap (navy crown, black peak, tiny brass cog badge) pops on with an overshoot when he's handed a schedule, and he salutes. The logistics cap (cardboard flat cap with a tape band) is for shopkeeping. When he's startled, his lids spring up and knock the cap up off his head for a beat.
- **The swivel.** The collar cog turns when he swivels. He can turn ±100° to wave at players beside the track or the counter, while the foot stays square to the block.
- **The gimbal spring.** It's why he wobbles: the train's starts, stops and curves rock him on it, so he moves with the train even when he's holding still.
- **Steam puffs.** Each toot puffs a tiny pale-grey cloud from the whistle (a particle in game, a scaled puff prop in the preview).
- **Hands on the controls.** His claws close on the actual lever handles of Create's Train Controls in front of him and push or pull them in time with the train.

## Personality
*Proud, punctual, a bit fussy*: a stickler for the timetable who adores his job. He checks his watch far more often than he needs to, beams when the train pulls out, is mortified when he's lost, sulks when someone else drives, and is a polite, attentive shopkeeper. Off duty he's bored, a bit fidgety, and perks up the moment someone comes by.

## Face and Expressions
Moving parts: as Trade Bot (eye pods roll and pitch on their own; lids slide and tilt like brows; irises spin, scale, switch off; the head pitches, yaws and rolls; the neck telescopes), plus the whistle pipe (lag and droop) and the cap (pop on, pop off, tip back, knocked askew).

| Expression | How it reads |
|---|---|
| Neutral | Pods level, lids over the top third of the lens |
| Proud (new) | Chin up (head pitch −8°), lids half down in a satisfied squint, body puffed (scale 1.04 on X/Z), whistle perked, whiskers perked up |
| Happy | Pods outer-edges-down ~10°, lids up, irises 1.2×, whistle perks, whisker tips lift |
| Focused (new, driving) | Lids low and level, pods pitched forward 5°, irises 0.9×, head forward |
| Curious | Head roll 15°, one pod higher, irises 1.25× |
| Startled | Neck up 2 px, lids fully open (knocking the cap up), irises 0.7×, whistle springs back |
| Sad | Whiskers droop; droopy Λ: pods ~12° outer-edges-down (never a V, which reads cross), lids half, head down, whistle droops |
| Cross / sulking | Whiskers bristle (a quick huff); lids lowered and tilted into a frown, pods pitched forward, head low; sulking adds folded arms and a turned-away swivel |
| Confused | Pods tilted to one side, one lid down and one up, irises shrink then widen, head tilted at the schedule card |
| Dizzy | Irises spin (the catchlight orbits), pods wobble out of phase, cap askew |
| Sleepy | Lids sink, head nods, neck sinks, irises off |

## Motion Set

**Motion feel: fluid, tilting, full of inertia, and moved by the train.** This applies to everything below.
- **Nothing moves linearly and nothing stops dead.** Every movement eases in, overshoots and settles with one or two decaying wobbles.
- **He is a stack of springs on a gimbal.** The foot and post are rigid. The body rides the gimbal spring, the head follows the body, and the eye pods, whistle, cord, cap and claws follow whatever carries them. Each link lags a beat (body +0.03 s, head +0.05 s, eyes +0.09 s, whistle +0.14 s, cord +0.18 s) and settles later than the one below.
- **The train is his biggest input.** On a moving train, the carriage's acceleration drives the gimbal: when the train pulls away he's pushed back first (inertia), then leans into it; when it brakes he pitches forward and braces on the levers; on curves he banks outward and then back. A constant tiny rattle (0.1 px, 8–10 Hz) runs through him while the train moves. In game this comes from the carriage's motion, on top of whatever clip is playing.
- **Tilt is still his main way of moving.** Lean, bank and rock on the gimbal; swivel on the collar for big turns. The head counter-tilts to keep the eyes level.
- **Weight:** the head is heavy and lags most on pitch; the arms are light; the whistle and its cord are loosest and settle last; the cap rides on the head and only moves when knocked.

**Driving** (in control of a train; the game picks the clip from the train state)
- **Ready** (`drive_ready`, 3 s loop): both claws resting on the lever handles, body leaning 6° toward the controls, small idle sway, blinks. Now and then he glances at his watch pocket.
- **Throttle up** (`throttle_up`, 2.4 s): a determined nod and the Focused face. The train's inertia pushes him back ~6°; then he pushes both levers forward, leans in ~10°, the whistle streams back, and he settles into the cruise pose.
- **Cruising** (`cruise`, 4 s loop): one claw on a lever, the other relaxed at his side. A slow look out to one side and back, the train rattle running through him, one blink. A small proud chin lift halfway through.
- **Braking** (`brake`, 2.4 s): pitches forward ~12° as the train slows, hauls both levers back, braces with his elbows out, whistle and cap whip forward; rocks back and settles.
- **Waiting at a station** (`station_wait`, 6 s loop): takes out the pocket watch, flicks the lid open, studies it, taps it, holds it to his ear, frowns at the timetable card, snaps it shut and pockets it. Then drums his claw on a lever.
- **Departing** (`depart`, 2.8 s): a last watch check and a satisfied nod; the left claw tugs the whistle cord twice (toot-toot, two steam puffs); a proud little wave out of the side window; both claws back on the levers.
- **Honk** (`honk`, 1.2 s, layered on top of anything): a quick tug of the cord, one puff.
- **No path** (`no_path`, 4.2 s, holds its last frame): he takes the schedule card from the clip, reads it, turns it round, turns it upside down, looks at the controls and back at the card, then a big shrug with claws up and the whistle drooping. The card goes back in its clip.
- **A player drives** (`manual_folded`, 3 s loop): he lets go, swivels 20° away, folds his arms with the Cross face, and gives a little huff every few seconds.
- **Paused, nobody at the controls** (`paused_point`, 3 s loop): taps twice on the controls' cover, points at his schedule card, looks round at the nearest player expectantly, taps again.
- **Crash or derailment** (`crash`, 3.4 s): thrown forward into the levers, the cap knocked askew, eyes spinning dizzy, a head shake, then he slumps into the sulk.
- **Sulking** (`sulk`, 4 s loop, until the train is back on the rails): cap askew, arms folded, droopy Λ eyes, whistle drooping, one sad sigh (the body sinks 0.5 px and lifts).
- **No schedule** (`no_schedule`, 3 s loop): claws on the levers but looking round, then holding out an open claw toward the nearest player ("well?"), then tapping the empty clip.
- **Handed a schedule** (`schedule_given`, 2.6 s): the card appears in the clip, he snaps upright, the train cap pops on with an overshoot, and he **salutes** with his right claw, beaming with the Proud face. Claws to the levers.
- **Schedule taken back** (`schedule_taken`, 2.2 s): a disappointed look at the empty clip, the cap lifts off and pops away, sad Λ eyes, and he lets go of the levers.
- **Passenger** (`passenger`, 4 s loop; not in control): swivelled 60–90° toward a window side, watching the world go by, head tracking slowly, an occasional wave out of the window.

**Shopkeeping** (beside a Stock Ticker; logistics cap on)
- **Shop idle** (`shop_idle`, 3 s loop): swivelled to face out from the ticker, claws clasped in front, attentive, small sway, blinks.
- **Greet** (`shop_greet`, 2.2 s): sees a customer, tips his cap, a little wave, claws clasped again.
- **Order placed** (`shop_order_nod`, 1.6 s): two crisp nods and a small bow from the gimbal.

**Off duty, idle loop and mannerisms** (no train and no shop)
- **Idle loop** (`idle`, 3 s seamless): gimbal sway ±2° roll and ±1.5° pitch, out of phase, a 0.25 px bob, a blink (every 3–6 s in game), the whistle and arms a beat behind.
- **Watches players**: head and eyes track the nearest player within 8 blocks, and the swivel follows once the head passes 45°.
- **Mannerisms** (one at random every 6–15 s; the driving ones also play in `drive_ready` and `cruise`):
  1. **Check the watch** (`check_watch`, 3 s): watch out, lid open, glance, snap shut, pocket it.
  2. **Polish a lens** (`polish_lens`, 2.5 s): as Trade Bot's.
  3. **Toot a tune** (`toot_tune`, 3 s; off duty only): three tiny half-tugs on the cord, three wisp puffs, with little bounces in time.
  4. **Straighten the cap** (`adjust_cap`, 2 s; only with a cap): both claws set the cap straight, a tug on the peak.
  5. **Dust the controls** (`dust_controls`, 2.5 s; at the controls): a quick wipe of the lever handles, a blow on them.
  6. **Doze off** (`doze_off`, 6.8 s; off duty only): as Trade Bot's, with a jolt awake and a look round.

**Reactions** (anywhere)
- **Sees a player** (`sees_player`, 2.8 s): double-take with the neck shooting up, swivels to face them, waves.
- **Right-clicked off duty** (`wave`, 2 s): a big happy wave with the whistle perked.
- **Hostile mob nearby** (`hostile_shrink`, 1.6 s → `hostile_cower`, 2 s loop → `hostile_recover`, 2.2 s): eyes wide and irises pinpricks, then he makes himself small without leaving: post down 2 px, neck in, arms up in front of his chest, claws over his lenses, a fast tremble (9–13 Hz). Recovery: a peek, a look left and right, a relieved shake.
- **Rain on him** (`rain_wipe`, 2.2 s): a claw sweeps across both lenses left and right like a windscreen wiper, then a little shake.
- **Thunder** (`thunder_flinch`, 1.4 s): a jolt down on the gimbal, lids shut, then a nervous glance up.

**Ducking** (a solid block above him; pure pose, not a reaction)
- **Duck down** (`duck_down`, 1.0 s) and **duck up** (`duck_up`, 1.0 s): the post telescopes 3 px into the foot, the gimbal spring compresses 1 px, the neck retracts 2 px, so the pods' top drops from y22 to y16. The whistle pipe folds back flat along the left pod; the train cap tips back to hang on the back of his head. Coming up, everything springs back with one overshoot.
- **Ducked idle** (`idle_ducked`, 3 s loop): the idle at the lower height with a slightly squashed, cheeky look (lids half, a glance up at the ceiling now and then). Every other clip plays ducked by adding the duck offsets in game; arm poses that reach the levers are re-solved for the lower shoulders.

**Expressions as clips** (`expr_happy`, `expr_proud`, `expr_focused`, `expr_curious`, `expr_startled`, `expr_sad`, `expr_cross`, `expr_confused`, `expr_dizzy`): spring in, hold with the idle running, spring out, face parts arriving in a staggered chain. The game blends these over the current clip.

## Rig Requirements
Pivots in model pixels; origin at the centre of his foot plate, which sits **2 px forward of the block centre** (toward the controls, so his claws reach the lever handles); the foot still fits inside the block; x is his right, y is up, **his front faces −Z** (toward the controls). Head, eye, lid, iris and arm bones copy Trade Bot's so his generator code can be shared; only the lower body changes.

**Lit parts**: the irises only, as separate lit cubes over dark ones. **Tint**: the dye-zone faces (chest and side panels, forearms) use their own texture region, painted as greyscale mottling at mid value, so the renderer multiplies them by the dye colour; plain uses the andesite colours directly.

**Preview props** (Blockbench only, not in the mod): a **Train Controls stand-in** in the block in front (−Z), with its two lever handles at the positions measured from Create's controls model; a **ceiling block** above him for the duck clips; a **Stock Ticker stand-in** beside him for the shop clips; a **steam puff** (pale grey `#9a9c98`, scaled in and out) at the whistle mouth. Each prop appears only in its own clips and is parked out of view in the others.

## Fun Score (1-10)
**9**: he's Trade Bot's face doing a real job you can watch: hands on the actual levers, rocked by the train's own starts and stops, a watch he can't stop checking, a whistle he loves to pull, and a cap-on salute when you hand him the schedule. Players will stand on the platform to see him leave.
