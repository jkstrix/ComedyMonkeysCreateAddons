# Design Brief: Clockwork Juggernaut

Character brief for the Clockwork Juggernaut, the mod's first boss: design, motion, rig and preview clips. It also covers the props that come with him: the Winding Key block, the Mainspring item and the Spring Engine block.
Gameplay: [design/specs/clockwork-juggernaut.md](../specs/clockwork-juggernaut.md). Family reference: [Trade Bot brief](trade-bot.md) and [Helper Bot brief](helper-bot.md), with [trade-bot.gen.js](../models/trade-bot.gen.js) and [helper-bot.gen.js](../models/helper-bot.gen.js).
Model (to build): `design/models/clockwork-juggernaut.bbmodel`, generated by `design/models/clockwork-juggernaut.gen.js`.

## Revision 2 (2026-10-27): an ancient Norse construct

The user's art direction after the first blockout ("Needs more detail, needs to look more expressive", then a full note: broad near-triangular shoulders, larger brutal forearms and fists, a slight hunch "like it's been walking for centuries", thicker legs "like a living siege engine"; ancient Nordic constructs and dwarven craftsmanship). Approved in Blockbench ("approved to animate"). **Where this section and the ones below disagree, this section wins.** The design is original, in a mythic Norse style; it copies no game's assets.

- **Size:** 7 blocks tall to the top of the key, about 6 wide across the shields; the hitbox matches ("widen hitbox to match").
- **Silhouette:** an inverted triangle. Layered round shields on iron shoulder caps over wide-set shoulder joints (axle stubs, "widen his shoulder joints to help prevent clipping"); a broad chest over a narrow belly; a 17° hunch; thick legs, stone below the knee.
- **Palette** (replaces the Theme table):

  | Charcoal black | `#1c1e21` `#232629` `#2a2d31` |
  | Dark iron, blue-grey undertones | `#343a42` `#3d444d` `#454d56`, edge `#56606b` |
  | Weathered steel (armour plates) | `#4a525c` `#535c66`, rim `#626c77` |
  | Tarnished bronze (was brass) | `#5c4a2e` `#6e5a36` `#7d6a44`, patina `#4f6b5e` `#5b7a6c` |
  | Ancient gold, inlays only (the key, the seal, the nose-guard rivet) | `#a8873e` `#93763a` |
  | Stone, cracked and stapled | `#4e504d` `#5a5c58`, crack `#141516` |

- **Head** (replaces the eye pods, lids, visor and portcullis jaw): a carved stone **mask** with **one narrow glowing slit** behind an iron **nose guard** (a gold rivet); two **brow plates** set in a scowl (they carry the expressions); a band of **glowing runes** across the forehead; a domed helm with a bronze crest; cheek plates; and an **iron beard** of hanging bars with two bronze braid rings. The beard is the **steam vent**: a glow plate behind it lights for the vent's tell (it replaces the chest grille).
- **Armour:** **Viking-shield pauldrons** (a big shield behind a smaller one, bronze rims and bosses, plank lines, knotwork, stapled cracks; his left back shield is a later bronze replacement); **pectoral plates** with knotwork and runes (shed in stage 2, revealing cracked iron with rune-light beneath); vambraces (the left one stone) and greaves with knee caps (shed in stage 3).
- **Hands:** a brutal iron gauntlet on the right (three jointed fingers and a thumb, half-curled, for the grab), a huge cracked **stone fist** on the left with iron knuckle studs and a band.
- **Storytelling:** chains wrapped round the left forearm (a loose end hangs) and round the waist; leather straps and buckles holding plates and repairs together; frost along top edges and in the joints; cracks with rune-light under them; a **forge-maker's seal** (a hammer over an anvil, ringed in gold) hanging on his belly.
- **Kept:** the giant bronze **winding key** (now with a gold inlay) as the crown on his back, the spring drum, the pawl, the steam pipes, the cog joints and the cog rack: he is still clockwork, still the bots' ancestor.
- **Rig changes** (the model is the reference): `eye_*`, `lid_*`, `iris_*` and `visor` are replaced by `slit` → `slit_glow`, `brow_left`, `brow_right`, `helm` (stage 3 shed) and `jaw` (with the beard bars and `beard_glow`); `grille` is gone; pauldrons ride the arms (`arm_*` → `pauldron_*` → `shield_*`); fingers have `_tip` sub-bones; `chain_end` hangs from the left forearm; `waist_ring` sits on the hips.
- **Changes made to stop clipping** (every clip passes `clockwork-juggernaut.collide.js`, which checks each part against every other part):
  - the shoulders are **cog drums** (r 11) centred on the arm's pivot, so they look the same at any angle. The shields rest above the drums and turn back only when the arm swings out sideways more than 30°;
  - the elbows hinge at the **front** of the arm;
  - the hip, knee, ankle and waist joints are **round**;
  - the girdle is thinner and the calves taper;
  - the forearms and hands are 6 px shorter, so his hands hang at knee height;
  - the cog rack sits at the middle of his back, and the key's bow is at 56 px;
  - in walk and charge the feet tilt no more than ±13°, and the root is lifted so his feet always touch the ground.

## Hard Requirements

From the user:
- "hostile, big, hard to kill but non destructive"
- A huge clockwork war automaton, "the clockwork juggernaut", **summoned with a clockwork ritual** like the Wither
- "his attack cycle should be more varied"

From the spec:
- **"he wants to be twice as big as a wither"**: **7 blocks tall, about 4 wide** (user, 2026-10-27; D20). Built at full size, so his pixels match the world (D53).
- **The bots' ancestor**: Trade Bot and Helper Bot family look, built for war, long abandoned (D4).
- **A giant winding key on his back**, the weak spot: it's exposed while he rewinds and turns slowly, and hits on it from behind do 1.5× (D3, D23).
- **The rhythm has to be readable.** While **running down** he sags, his ticking slows and his **eyes dim**. While **rewinding** he stands still with the key spinning. On a **feint** his eyes **stay lit** and a sharp click sounds. A **key jam** throws sparks and grinds (D3, D47, D48).
- **Three stages.** At 67% he sheds his **chest and shoulder plates**; at 33% he sheds **the rest**. Shed plates are clatter and particles only (D43).
- **Moves**:
  - charge;
  - sweeping arm swing;
  - stomp shockwave, doubled in stage 3;
  - grab and throw (1 s while the hand opens);
  - spring leap (1.5 s coil);
  - steam vent from a **chest grille** (2 s hiss while the grille glows);
  - gear spin (arms out, 2 s wind-up, 4 s spin);
  - cog throw (D44, D45, D22).
- **States**: waking (about 3 s), wound, running down, rewinding, dormant (**kneeling**, mending), and waking from a ruin (eyes light, ticking starts) (D29, D32).
- **Ruins**: dormant ones **kneel under vines** in the Overworld, and **under chorus plants, half-buried in End Stone** on the End's outer islands (D31, D55).
- **The effigy is four Brass Blocks in a T, with the Winding Key as a block on the back of the T's centre, turned by a shaft** (D16, D18).
- The **Spring Engine** takes a shaft in at its back and gives a shaft out at its front. It may have to be **two blocks** (one kinetic network each) (D35, D36, Technical Notes).

Standing art rules (user):
- dark and muted, mid grey at most;
- accents sparingly;
- big industrial things get real relief hardware;
- preview in Blockbench before anything goes into the mod.

## Asset Type

1. **Clockwork Juggernaut**: character (hostile boss mob, an entity).
   - **64 px wide across the shoulders, 112 px tall to the top of the key bow** (7 blocks, twice the Wither), about 52 px deep including the key. Built at **full size** (1 px = 1/16 block), not a small model drawn larger as the Wither is, so his pixels match Create's blocks around him.
   - `modded_entity` format (box UV), code-driven animation from the preview timings (no GeckoLib), like the bots.
   - One generator script for geometry, texture, rig and clips. A 1024 px texture: he's about six times Trade Bot's height and keeps his mottling at the same pixel density. At this size there's room for real-part detail the bots can't carry (cog teeth, rivet rows, buckles, dents, hinge pins), in the spirit of the Cuckoo Clock: rich, but every detail a real part.
2. **Winding Key**: block (1×1), placed on a Brass Block face, with a shaft socket. Rendered with its key turning (a block-entity renderer or a Flywheel visual).
3. **Mainspring**: item, a flat 16×16 icon.
4. **Spring Engine**: kinetic block, **1×1×2**, lying along its shaft axis: winding end at the back, output end at the front. If one block turns out to be enough in code, compress the same design to 1×1×1 (see Builder Notes).

## Fantasy

**A siege engine from the age before the bots**: the first clockwork soldier, wound up for a war nobody remembers and left kneeling in the dark for centuries. Wind him up and he goes straight back to work, relentless, until his spring runs out.

## Hero Element

**The giant winding key.** A two-loop brass key, its bow **wider than his head**, rising behind his shoulders like a crown.
- It's the silhouette's signature: it's seen first from every side, including dead ahead, where the loops show above his shoulders.
- It's the fight's tell. It turns steadily while he's wound, slows as he runs down, and spins fast while he rewinds.
- It's the target players aim for.
- It's the family link: Trade Bot has a little key on his back (Helper Bot lost his), and this is the ancestor's, huge.

The bow is muted brass with dark wear in its loops. A heavy steel collar where the stem enters his back drum carries the ratchet teeth that spark on a jam. Nothing else on him is that size of brass.

## Theme

The same robot family and the same Create materials, **aged and grim**: rust-dark steel, blackened brass, a little verdigris on the copper. He'll be seen beside Brass Blocks (his effigy), Create's machinery, the Netherite Monster Cage and the bots.

| Material | Where | Colours |

| Old dark steel | frame, limbs, head, back drum | `#2a2e33` `#343940` `#3f454c`, rust shading `#4a3a30` in the crevices |
| Armour plates | helm visor, chest plate, pauldrons, forearm and shin guards | weathered steel `#40464d` `#4c535b`, a few dents (darker pixels), rivets in muted brass `#8f7638` |

The family glows tell them apart: Trade Bot amber, Helper Bot cyan, Juggernaut **ember**. No hazard stripes, no saturated surfaces. His danger comes from shape and motion, not colour.

## Shape Language

**Squares dominate**: heavy, industrial, immovable. He's a stack of armoured boxes, with stepped bevels on every big edge so he never looks like a crate.
- **Circles** carry the family and the clockwork: round eye lenses, the round chest grille, joint cogs, round feet, the key's loops, the cogs he throws.
- **Triangles** are the aggression accents, used sparingly:
  - the helm visor's downward wedge over the eyes (a permanent scowl);
  - the pointed top edges of the pauldrons;
  - the ram-like prow of the chest plate.

## Silhouette Description

**A hunched gorilla-shaped boiler with a key for a crown.**
- **Front:** a wide trapezoid, with massive shoulders (64 px) tapering to narrow hips (32 px) on two short pillar legs. Long arms hang to his knees. A small helmeted head is sunk low between the shoulders, and the key's two loops rise above and behind it. Reads like a capital **M** with a key handle on top.
- **Side:** a heavy forward hunch. The head is ahead of the chest, the arms hang forward, the back drum is round, and the key sticks out behind at a slight upward angle.
- **Kneeling (dormant):** a low mound, about 60 px (4 blocks) tall. One knee down, both fists on the ground, head bowed, the key upright like a gravestone handle.
- **Stage changes have to read in silhouette.**
  - Stage 2: the pauldrons are gone, so the shoulders narrow to bare cog joints (about 52 px). The chest plate is gone and the round glowing grille shows.
  - Stage 3: thinner limbs and an exposed, angrier face. He looks like a machine falling apart and fighting harder for it.

## Primary Forms

1. **Torso** (the boiler): a barrel-chested box, 40 px wide, 36 tall, 28 deep, at y44–80, with stepped corners. His bulk leans forward ~15° at the waist.
2. **Back drum and key**:
   - **Drum:** a spring barrel across his upper back, 28 px across, 16 px deep, round-ish (stepped), steel with brass bands.
   - **Key stem:** 8×8 px, from the drum's centre, angled 15° up, 10 px long.
   - **Key bow:** two loops of 20×20 px with 4 px thick brass bars, 44 px across and 4 px thick. It turns in the plane of his back.
3. **Shoulders and arms**: huge cog shoulder joints (16 px Create-style cogs) at y68–84 carry long arms. Upper arm 12×24, forearm 16×28, which is thicker: he's a Popeye brute.
   - **Right hand**: a big three-finger brass-tipped claw, the family claw grown up. It's for grabbing.
   - **Left hand**: a heavy round fist with a cog-ring knuckle guard, for stomping the ground and swinging.
4. **Legs**: short pillar legs, the thigh 14×16 and the shin 16×18, on round stepped feet (20 px across). Knees are cog joints. Hips are a narrow steel girdle at y32–44.

## Secondary Forms

- **Head**: a small 20×16×16 helmeted box sunk between the shoulders, y80–96.
  - **Eyes:** the family binocular eye pods, scaled down relative to his body, deep-set behind a **visor slit**: two round 8×8 lenses in blackened-brass rims with ember irises.
  - **Lids:** heavy steel shutter plates.
  - **Jaw:** a **portcullis grille** under the eyes (vertical steel bars), his voice and exhaust.
- **Helm visor** (armour, shed in stage 3): a wedge plate over the brow, angled down into a scowl.
- **Chest plate** (armour, shed in stage 2): a prow-shaped plate over the chest grille, with rivet rows and a few dents.
- **Pauldrons** (armour, shed in stage 2): layered shoulder plates (two overlapping lames each) with pointed top edges.
- **Forearm guards and shin guards** (armour, shed in stage 3): slab plates strapped on with brass buckles.
- **Chest grille** (revealed in stage 2): a round 16 px copper-ringed grille with horizontal slots and the ember glow behind it. The steam vent comes from here.
- **Exposed clockwork** (revealed as plates fall):
  - behind the pauldrons, the shoulder cog trains;
  - behind the shin guards, piston rods;
  - behind the visor, the full eye pods and their brass rims.
  
  Each is a real mechanism that moves with the joint.
- **Cog rack**: a copper peg on his right hip holding three stacked cogs (12 px). It's where the thrown cogs come from: the claw takes one, and the rack shows one fewer for a moment (it refills, for readability).
- **Steam pipes**: two short copper pipes from the back drum, curving over the shoulders to vents behind the head. They puff on stomps, charges and the vent tell.

## Interesting Features

- **The key tells you everything.** Steady while wound, slower and lazier as he runs down, a blur while he rewinds, shuddering and sparking when jammed. A player can read the fight from the key alone.
- **His eyes are the feint tell.** A real run-down dims them in steps with the ticks. A feint keeps them lit, and the lids snap open with the sharp click.
- **He comes apart as you win.** Each stage strips armour off with a clatter, revealing moving clockwork and a glowing chest. He looks more dangerous and more vulnerable at once.
- **He salutes.** On waking, after the unfold, he snaps a stiff military salute with the claw, a soldier reporting for duty. He salutes again over a player he's just defeated. It's the one beat of charm in a grim character, and it echoes Helper Bot's salute.
- **He dies like a toy running down.** His last wind-down slows to a stop, he drops to one knee, the key spins loose, and the **Mainspring bursts out of his chest** with a twang, uncoiling in the air. That's the drop, made visible.
- **The ritual effigy shakes as you wind it.** The T of Brass Blocks rattles in time with the ticking, and steam escapes from the seams. On waking the effigy and key unfold into him, in a flash of particles plus his waking clip. Underground, the effigy collapses into particles and a column of steam instead, and he appears on the surface above, kneeling (`dormant`).

## Personality

*Relentless, proud, weary*: an old soldier still following orders nobody remembers. He fights with grim discipline, and every move is a drill. Under it he's tired: he sags as he runs down like a man at the end of a long march, and kneels to rest when nobody's left to fight. He respects nothing except a worthy opponent, so he salutes the one he's beaten.

## Face and Expressions

Moving parts:
- the eye pods (pitch, small roll);
- the shutter lids (slide; tilt like brows);
- the irises (scale, dim, off);
- the visor (only until it's shed);
- the jaw grille (drops 2 px to "breathe" steam);
- the head (pitch, yaw, roll, limited by the shoulders).

| Expression | How it reads |
|---|---|
| Neutral (wound) | Lids at a hard half-close under the visor, irises full, head low and forward |
| Fury (charge, gear spin, stage change) | Lids slam down to a narrow slit tilted into a V, irises 1.2× and brighter, head drops, jaw grille vents |
| Tired (running down) | Lids sink step by step with each tick, irises dim in steps, head droops and rolls to one side |
| Rewinding | Head bowed forward to present the key, lids shut, irises nearly off; a faint glow pulses with the key |
| Alert (feint snap, waking) | Lids spring fully open, irises flare, head snaps up |
| Jammed | Head jerks to one side, one lid open and one shut, irises flicker |
| Dormant | Lids shut, irises off, head bowed; a faint ember flicker every few seconds, like breathing |
| Salute | Lids level and steady, head up: proud, for once |

## Motion Set

**Motion feel: heavy, mechanical, with clockwork stops and a spring under everything.** This applies to everything below.
- **He's a heavy stack of springs that moves in ticks.** His big moves are fluid and weighty: a long anticipation, a heavy follow-through, slow settles. The heavy parts lag most: torso +0.06 s, head +0.08 s, arms +0.1 s, key bow +0.12 s, steam pipes +0.16 s.
- **Small adjustments step instead of gliding**, like an escapement: the head turning to track a player, the lids, the fingers. Discrete 2–3° ticks make him read as clockwork rather than flesh.
- **Tilt is how he moves.** He leans hard into a charge, rocks back on stopping, and the whole body twists into a swing. Each stomp squashes him (−2 px at the hips) and shakes the plates.
- **Speed tracks his spring.** At full wind, moves play at full speed. While running down they slow (to 0.7× by the end), and in stage 3 everything plays 1.15× faster with sharper settles.
- **Loose parts:** the pauldron lames, buckles and steam pipes rattle a beat after every impact.

### Locomotion
- **Walk** (`walk`, 1.6 s loop): a heavy, wide-legged march.
  - Each step lands with a 1 px body dip and a forward-and-back torso rock.
  - The arms swing opposite to the legs, the claw opening a little on the back swing.
  - The key turns steadily, and the steam pipes bob a beat behind.
- **Run-down walk**: the same clip at 0.7× with an added slump (torso +6° forward, head roll 5°).
- **Turn**: he plants a foot and swivels at the hips in steps; the torso follows, then the head.

### Idle
**Idle** (`idle`, 3.0 s loop):
- His weight shifts from foot to foot and the torso breathes (0.6 px).
- The key turns, the head ticks left and right as if scanning, and the claw fingers flex once.

### Mannerisms
Only while wound with no target in reach, so they're rare:
- **Sentry scan** (`scan`, 2.5 s): the head sweeps in ticks, stops, then the lids narrow at nothing.
- **Knuckle check** (`knuckles`, 2.0 s): he flexes the left fist, and the cog ring on the knuckles turns once.
- **Steam sigh** (`sigh`, 2.0 s): the shoulders rise and fall, and both pipes puff.

### Attacks
Each attack has a clear anticipation, so a player can learn to read it.

1. **Charge** (`charge`, 2.2 s):
   - **Anticipation** (0.6 s): he lowers his head, scrapes his left foot back twice and leans forward ~20°; the pipes puff and the lids slam to fury.
   - **Run:** a run loop at a 25° lean, with the claw and fist forward like a battering ram.
   - **Recovery** (0.5 s): he skids to a stop and rocks back.
2. **Swing** (`swing`, 1.4 s):
   - **Anticipation** (0.4 s): the torso twists 40° back, winding the left fist behind him.
   - **The swing:** a sweeping 120° arc at chest height.
   - **Follow-through:** an overshoot twist and a settle.
3. **Stomp** (`stomp`, 1.3 s) and **double stomp** (`stomp_double`, 2.0 s):
   - **Anticipation** (0.5 s): he raises his right leg high, the body leans back and the arms go out for balance.
   - **Impact:** the foot slams down, the body squashes 3 px, and the plates jump.
   - **The ring:** the shockwave is a particle ring in game, and a flat ring prop in the preview.
   - **Double stomp:** the second stomp comes from the left foot 0.5 s after the first.
4. **Grab and throw** (`grab_throw`, 2.4 s):
   - **Anticipation** (1.0 s, per spec): the right claw rises and opens wide, fingers splayed, and the head tilts to aim.
   - **Grab:** the claw snaps shut at chest height.
   - **Hold** (0.4 s): he holds the claw up and in front of his face, inspecting his catch.
   - **Throw:** an overhead hurl.
   - **Settle:** a heavy settle.
   - **Prop:** the preview shows a player-sized prop in the claw.
   - **Shield-block variant** (`grab_blocked`, 1.2 s): the claw clangs shut on nothing and he recoils, shaking it.
5. **Spring leap** (`leap`, 2.6 s):
   - **Coil** (1.5 s, per spec): the legs bend deep, the body squashes 6 px, the key spins up and the steam pipes hiss.
   - **Launch:** a stretched launch, then he flies with the arms raised together like a hammer.
   - **Landing:** a huge squash with both fists into the ground, a long rattle, and a slow rise.
6. **Steam vent** (`steam_vent`, 3.0 s):
   - **Tell** (2.0 s, per spec): he plants his feet, the shoulders roll back and the chest swells 1 px. The grille glows up from dark red to ember, and the jaw grille drops.
   - **Blast** (0.8 s): he leans into it, and the steam cone is particles in game.
   - **After:** the steam gutters and he shakes his head.
7. **Gear spin** (`gear_spin_start` 2.0 s, `gear_spin` 1.0 s loop, `gear_spin_end` 1.5 s):
   - **Wind-up:** the arms go straight out, the torso winds back the other way, and the key spins hard.
   - **Spin:** the torso and arms spin at the waist like a governor, the arms lifting outward with speed, the legs braced, and the head lagging, then whipping round.
   - **End:** he staggers out of the spin, dizzy for a beat (the irises wobble out of phase), then shakes it off.
8. **Cog throw** (`cog_throw`, 1.6 s): the claw reaches to the hip rack and takes a cog, his weight shifts back, and he throws sidearm with a grunt of steam. The rack shows one cog fewer until the clip's end.

### Rhythm states
- **Running down** (`run_down`, 4.0 s, holds its last frame; it's the last 4 s of each wind):
  - The key slows to a stop over four escapement ticks spaced further and further apart (0.4, 1.3, 2.3, 3.5 s). Each tick drops him further into a slump.
  - With each tick the torso goes forward, the arms go limp, the head droops and rolls, the lids sink and the irises dim one step.
  - It's Trade Bot's `wind_down` in a giant.
- **Rewinding** (`rewind`, loop, 1.0 s cycle; the game holds it 4–6 s plus any jam):
  - He stands stock still, bowed forward (torso 25°, head down), presenting the key to anyone behind.
  - The key spins fast (720°/s), with a faint pulse in the irises at each turn.
  - At the end (`rewind_end`, 1.0 s) he straightens with a shudder, the lids open, and the irises come back up.
- **Feint** (`feint`, 1.8 s):
  - It plays like the first two ticks of `run_down`, but **the eyes never dim**.
  - Then a sharp snap: the lids spring open, the head snaps up, and he lunges forward into a short swipe with the claw.
- **Key jam** (`key_jam`, 1.2 s, layered over `rewind`): the key catches and shudders, the collar sparks, his whole body jerks twice, and his head jerks to one side (one lid open, one shut).
- **Stage change** (`shed_plates_2`, `shed_plates_3`, 1.6 s each):
  - **Stagger:** the stagger the spec asks for: he reels back a step, arms out.
  - **Plates fly:** the plates for that stage spring off, flung outward and spinning. In game they're particles with a clatter. In the preview they're props that tumble and park out of view.
  - **Roar:** he roars a burst of steam from the jaw grille, and his lids go to fury.

### Reactions
- **Hit while wound** (`hit_wound`, 0.4 s): barely a shudder. The plates ring, he ticks his head toward the attacker, and the lids narrow.
- **Hit while rewinding** (`hit_open`, 0.6 s): a real flinch. The body jolts forward, the key wobbles on its axis, and the irises flicker.
- **Lightning rewound** (`lightning`, 1.2 s): he jolts upright mid-rewind, arcs crawl over the key, and the irises flare. He's straight back into the fight.

### Rest, waking and death
- **Dormant** (`dormant`, 4.0 s loop): kneeling on one knee with both fists down and his head bowed. The key is still. A faint ember flicker in the irises every few seconds, and the odd steam wisp.
- **Waking** (`wake`, 3.0 s, starts from `dormant`):
  1. The irises light in turn and the key gives one heavy turn.
  2. He pushes up off his fists, unfolding joint by joint in steps.
  3. He stands, the plates settle with a rattle, and the pipes puff.
  4. He snaps a salute with the claw (0.5 s hold), then drops into the wound idle.
- **Ritual wake** (`wake_ritual`, 3.0 s): the same as `wake` but starting from a standing crouch, in the T's place. He uncurls and spreads his arms, then salutes.
- **Death** (`death`, 4.0 s):
  1. A final wind-down, three ticks, the eyes going out one by one.
  2. He drops to one knee, then forward onto both fists.
  3. The key spins free and backwards, then the **Mainspring bursts out of his chest** with an uncoiling twang (a prop: a flat spiral that springs out and spins).
  4. A last puff of steam.
- **Victory salute** (`salute`, 2.0 s): when a player dies near him, he stands straight and salutes, then goes back to scanning.

## Spring Engine (block)

**A clock's spring barrel, scaled up into a Create machine, lying along its shaft.** Size 1×1×2: the back block winds, the front block gives.

- **The barrel:** a round (stepped) dark-steel drum, 12 px across, running the length, with three **brass bands**.
- **The spring window:** a slot window down one side, between the bands, shows the **coiled mainspring inside**. The renderer draws the coil **tight and many-turned when wound, loose and open when empty**. That's how you read it, so don't add a dial (the user's "no duplicate dials" rule).
- **The back block (winding):**
  - an andesite casing end with the input shaft;
  - a **ratchet wheel and click pawl** on its face, which tick while winding;
  - the input-stress value box.
- **The front block (output):**
  - a brass casing end with the output shaft;
  - a small **escapement wheel** that turns while it unwinds;
  - the speed value box.
- **Fallback:** if the code allows one block, the barrel shortens to 16 px. The ratchet and the escapement then sit on opposite end faces, and nothing else changes.
- **Restraint:** brass only on the bands, ratchet and escapement. No glow, no stripes.
