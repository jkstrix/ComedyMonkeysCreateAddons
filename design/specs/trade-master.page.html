Trade Master

The Trade Bot, upgraded and bearded. Stick Factory Gauges on your chests and he keeps them full by trading with villagers: he buys what's missing and sells your surplus to pay for it.

Design ready: 85% Late game 5×5 upgrade of a Trade Bot Look: modelled, in review

In ten lines

  • Upgraded Trade Bot with a beard. Costs a lot.
  • Tune him to a Create logistics network.
  • Boards on every chest he uses. They're cheap.
  • Put Factory Gauges on boards: "keep N here".
  • Low gauge: fetch owned stock, then buy the rest.
  • Pays with spare emeralds, or sells surplus first.
  • Never sells or spends below any gauge.
  • Never pays above base price. Restocks still limit him.
  • Carries by hand, 64 blocks, seated villagers only.
  • No board on a chest? He never touches it.

How you use him

  1. Upgrade a Trade Bot

    5×5 mechanical crafter, with a new Trade Ledger. He keeps the old bot's reputation.

  2. Tune and place

    Right-click a Stock Link with him, then place his station. FE from below.

  3. Fit Trade Boards

    On every chest he takes from or fills. Cheap, and no Packagers needed.

  4. Add gauges

    Factory Gauges on the boards he fills: "2 stacks of bookshelves", "4 stacks of emeralds".

  5. Seat villagers

    Sellers of what you want, buyers of what you have spare.

How a gauge gets filled

Every chest he uses has a Trade Board. No gauges: he takes from it. Gauges: he fills it to what they ask. No Packagers, and chests without a board don't exist for him.

  • FirstFetch it if you already own itHe never buys what's sitting in another chest.
  • ThenPay with spare emeraldsOnly emeralds above any emerald gauge. That gauge is your savings floor.
  • If shortSell the most plentiful thingNever below its own gauges. Sells only when emeralds are needed.
  • LastBuy, deliver, tidy upExtra goes in the gauge's chest. Change goes back where it came from.

Recipes

The Trade Master costs a Trade Bot, roughly twice that again in Create parts, and 4 netherite. Trade Boards stay cheap, because you'll want one on every chest he fills.

Rules

He does

  • Fill gauges on Trade Boards tuned to his network
  • Use stock you already own before trading
  • Sell anything in his board chests above its gauges
  • Keep the Trade Bot's reputation and charge
  • Work unattended wherever his area is loaded
  • Go home at night, in storms, and to charge

He doesn't

  • Listen to gauges that aren't on a Trade Board
  • Take anything below a gauge, to sell or to spend
  • Pay prices that demand has pushed above base
  • Trade with villagers who aren't seated
  • Need Packagers or packages: he carries everything himself
  • Touch chests without a Trade Board

What if…?

Nobody in range sells it?The gauge is listed as "nobody sells this". He doesn't go looking.
The board chest is too far away?Listed as "out of range". He never goes there.
No spare emeralds and nothing to sell?He waits. The gauge stays missing.
A trade gives more than needed?The extra goes into that chest anyway. Gauges are floors.
A board is broken mid-trip?Goods go back where they came from. Nothing is lost.
Two Trade Masters want one gauge?First come, first served. The other picks another gauge.
Prices went up after heavy trading?He skips that offer until a restock.
He'd sell my diamonds?Only if they're in a board chest with no gauge for them. Keep them in a chest without a board.
A trade needs a book as well?He fetches the book from his board chests too, or skips the trade.
Where does trade XP go?As Nuggets of Experience, to a board chest with a nugget gauge. Banked until there is one.
