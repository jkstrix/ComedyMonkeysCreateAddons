Clockwork Wastes

A dead clockwork city of unknown make, out on the End's big islands. Wrench its wrecks for parts. Bring power and its streets light up, its vaults unlock, and its sleeping bots wake to fight. Light the heart for the Elytra, and for the parts to turn a Nether Star into 8 hours of power.

In ten lines

  • Dead clockwork cities on one End island in ten.
  • Never near End cities.
  • Wrench wrecks for parts; pickaxe gives only scrap.
  • 1M FE into a Substation wakes its district.
  • Waking opens locks and wakes the bots.
  • Cut its power: lamps and Sentries go dark.
  • Four districts awake wakes the Custodian.
  • Break its four pylons, then kill it.
  • Its death opens the vault: Elytra inside.
  • Nether Star in a Star Reactor: 8h of power.

The city

About 144 blocks across: four districts round a heart. Each district has its own Substation, vault and bots. Cities stand 32 chunks apart, 3–4 to a Wastes patch, so the Elytra they hold match the End-city ships they replace.

Substation, one per district Derelict street lamps Heart: Elytra, Unknown Devices

One visit

  1. Find itA rust-brown haze and drifting brass dust out past the gateways.
  2. SalvageWrench broken machines and bot shells for Create parts and Clockwork Scrap.
  3. Wake a districtPut 1,000,000 FE into its Substation. A charged Battery does it.
  4. Fight, lootLamps ripple on, locks open, Sentries and Scuttlers wake. Clear them, open the vault.
  5. Light the heartWake all four, or one Nether Star in the heart wakes everything. The Custodian rises.
  6. Take it homeThe Elytra, plus Unknown Devices to build your own Star Reactor.

When a district wakes

LampsRipple on down the streets. Stay lit while fed 500 FE/t.
LocksDoors and the vault unlock for good, even if the power is cut later.
SentriesUnfold and fire bolts. Fold up again while the district is dark.
ScuttlersWake in packs of 3–5 and stay awake, power or not.

The Custodian

The heart's guardian, wired to the city's grid. Its death opens the heart vault. There's no grabbing the Elytra and running.

  1. It wakesFour districts awake: the heart's doors open and it rises from its dais.
  2. ShieldedWhile any of its four pylons stands, it takes no damage and mends.
  3. Short the pylons6 hits each, melee or arrows. Each one broken calls 2 Scuttlers.
  4. OverloadAt half health it speeds up, sparks, and re-lights one pylon. Break it again.
  5. DownThe vault clunks open: the Elytra and 2 Unknown Devices. It drops one more.

Rules

Does

  • Keep 6 chunks clear of End cities.
  • Put an Elytra in every city's heart vault.
  • Wake a district for good at 1M FE.
  • Fold Sentries while their district is dark.
  • Turn scrap into nuggets on Crushing Wheels.
  • Let a Deployer with a Wrench salvage, and arms or packages feed stars.
  • Run Star Reactors on airships.

Doesn't

  • Spawn on the central island.
  • Give free Electric Lights or Power Lines: city lamps break into scrap.
  • Let you break, blast or drill a locked vault or door.
  • Place a city on too little land: under half on End Stone, it's outskirts only.
  • Respawn its bots or refill its loot.
  • Wake bots on Peaceful.
  • Have shulkers: End cities are still the source.

What if…?

Two players power one Substation?Their FE adds up; it wakes for everyone.
The power gets cut after waking?Lamps go dark, Sentries fold, locks stay open.
A star lights the heart after some districts woke?Woken districts stay woken; nothing wakes twice.
Part of a district is unloaded when it wakes?The city's record holds it; those blocks catch up when loaded.
Everyone flees the Custodian?30 s later it resets: pylons re-lit, health full, vault still locked.
An admin /kills the Custodian?The vault opens, but it drops nothing.
Another End mod hides the biome?A warning in the log; a config option puts cities in vanilla highlands.
Someone digs round a locked door?The loot's inside the vault block, which can't be broken while locked.
A Star Reactor is broken mid-burn?It drops with the star's burn left inside.
A Wither farm feeds reactors?It gains power, held back by chow, the 20-minute pace and finite devices. Accepted.
Peaceful?Nothing wakes, but the locks still open.
