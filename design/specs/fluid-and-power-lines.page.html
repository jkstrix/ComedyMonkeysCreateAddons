Fluid Lines & Power Lines

Pump-free pipes for fluid and lines for power. They come in copper and brass, you can see their limits, you upgrade them by clipping on parts you can see, and they hide inside any block.

Design ready · 85% Replaces Pipez in this mod No items · no pump · no power

The whole idea in 10 lines

  • Fluid Lines for fluid, Power Lines for FE. No items.
  • No pump. No power. Just place it.
  • Fluid needs an Intake at the source.
  • Power flows by itself.
  • Copper is slow, brass is fast.
  • Copper and brass never join. Different colours join only if wrenched.
  • Upgrades are parts you clip on.
  • Power Lines clip flat to floors and walls, or hang between poles.
  • Wooden posts stack for height, with 1 to 4 branches on top.
  • Encase a line in any block to hide it.
  • Goggles show where it's hidden.

What it might look like

Rough sketches of the idea. The real models come from the art brief and get previewed in Blockbench first, in the mod's dark, muted style.

  • Thin and round. Fluid Lines are slim pipes, about 3–4 px across, with ring couplings. No square collars.
  • Wires look like wires. Power Lines are thin cable, even when placed: bare in their material, or a slightly thicker covered cable when dyed. They lie flat on the floor, wall or ceiling they were placed on, held down by clips.
  • Dye them. A line is just its colour: dyed all over, or its material when undyed. Nothing glows. Same colours join by themselves; different colours, undyed included, only join if you wrench them together.

The fittings

The upgrades. Each one clips onto a straight piece of line, one per block, and the wrench takes it off again. No menus or upgrade slots. Every one costs a real Create part.

How you use it

  1. Lay the lineFrom a tank or generator to the machines.
  2. Clip an IntakeOn the source side. Fluid only: power needs none.
  3. Watch it flowFluid moves past the window along the pipe. Read a Meter for power.
  4. Add controlValve, Filter, Meter or Splitter where you need it.
  5. Hide itEncase it in your walls. Goggles find it again.

What if…?

A chunk unloads mid-line?Those parts are skipped. Nothing is lost.
I break a line while it's flowing?Nothing spills. Lines store nothing.
The Intake's tank is also fed by the line?It never feeds itself.
A ship flies off with one end of a span?The span comes down and drops its wire.
Both span ends are on the ship?It stays up and keeps working.
Two fluids could flow?Set each Intake's filter; Filters split the line.
Someone builds through a span?The span stays. Blocks only matter when you hang it.
I still have Pipez covers?They keep working, but they're no longer craftable.

How ready is the design?

Still open

Defaults: approved

  • Intakes only draw from containers, never from world water or lava.
  • Hanging a span costs 1 Power Line per 4 blocks, refunded when it comes down.
  • Posts have no tier. Each insulator is its own connection, set by the first cable tied to it, up to 2 spans.
  • Add a branch by using a Line Post on the side of a head; the wrench takes it off.
  • Copper lines don't oxidise.
  • With no Splitter, a line shares evenly between its ends.
  • Set a Filter or an Intake with a bucket; an empty hand clears it.
  • The encasing block is kept and the wrench gives it back.
  • Goggles show hidden lines within 16 blocks.
  • Recipes as listed in the spec (8 lines per craft).

Parked for later

  • Item lines (left out of v1 on purpose).
  • Bare (uninsulated) span looks.
  • An adapter into Create's Fluid Pipes.
  • Spans that stretch between a moving ship and the ground.
