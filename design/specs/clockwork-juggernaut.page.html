Clockwork Juggernaut

A war automaton seven blocks tall, twice the Wither's height: the bots' grim ancestor. Wind him up and he wakes. While his spring is wound he shrugs off almost everything; then he stops to rewind, key exposed, and that's your moment. Every third of his health you take, he sheds plates, learns new moves and speeds up. He never breaks a block. Beat him for a Mainspring, the heart of the only block that stores rotation.

Design ready: 85% Late game · beside the Wither Summoned, or asleep in Overworld and End ruins Look: brief written

In ten lines

  • Brass T, Winding Key, turn it with a shaft: he wakes.
  • Wound: nearly unhurtable. Arrows bounce.
  • He stops to rewind, faster each stage.
  • Rewinding: hit him. The key from behind hurts most.
  • Seven blocks tall. Never breaks blocks.
  • Kill him: one Mainspring, always.
  • A Mainspring builds a Spring Engine.
  • Spring Engine stores rotation: shaft in, shaft out.
  • Full engine: an hour at 16,384 SU.
  • Can't be jarred. Also sleeps in rare ruins.

The ritual

  1. Craft a Winding Key

    Mechanical Crafter: a Netherite Ingot, 4 Precision Mechanisms, 4 Brass Sheets.

  2. Build the effigy

    Four Brass Blocks in a T, like the Wither's soul sand.

  3. Fit the key

    Place it on the back of the T's centre.

  4. Wind it

    A shaft at 64 RPM or more, 30 s without stopping. The ticking grows louder.

  5. He wakes

    Effigy and key are used up. Underground, he rises to the surface above and kneels there until you come up. Not in the Nether.

Read his rhythm

The whole fight is this cycle, tighter each stage. Survive the grey stretch, punish the brass one. A feint clicks sharply and his eyes stay lit; a real run-down dims them. Lightning skips his rewind.

The reward: Spring Engine

Create can't store rotation. This can. It makes nothing: every SU it gives back was wound in earlier.

  1. Build it

    Mainspring, Brass Casing, 4 Brass Sheets, 2 Shafts, at a crafting table.

  2. Wind it

    Shaft into the back. Set how much stress it takes: 1,024 SU by default, up to 16,384.

  3. Let it out

    Shaft out of the front, 1–256 RPM, 16,384 SU on offer. Drains only what's used.

  4. Take it with you

    Keeps its wind when broken. Works on Aeronautics ships: fuel-free power for a voyage.

Rules

He does

  • Charge, sweep and stomp, with big knockback
  • Throw a cog if he can't reach you for 5 s
  • Shed plates, learn moves and speed up at 67% and 33% health
  • Fake a run-down now and then, from stage 2
  • Sit down and mend if everyone leaves for 60 s
  • Always drop one Mainspring, brass, Precision Mechanisms, 50 XP

He doesn't

  • Break or change any block, ever
  • Burn, drown, suffocate, take fall damage, or take poison or wither
  • Take damage from saws, drills, crushers or deployers
  • Fit in a jar or a cage
  • Wake on peaceful
  • Drop extra Mainsprings with Looting

What if…?

The shaft stops mid-ritual?The key slowly unwinds back. Start again.
I walk near a ruin?5 s warning: his eyes light. Back off past 24 blocks and he settles.
A storm rolls in?Lightning rewinds him instantly. Fight under clear skies.
I lure him into lava?Immune. He'll walk the bottom of a lake too.
Everyone logs off mid-fight?Saved. He sits dormant and mends after 60 s alone.
I loop an engine into itself?Net zero. It drains by what's used, winding included.
I put an engine on a train?Carried, but still. Aeronautics ships only.
He sags, I run in, he lunges?A feint. Watch his eyes: lit means fake. The real rewind comes 5 s later.
He reaches for me?Raise a shield: no grab, no throw.
I summon him underground?He rises to the surface above and kneels until a player comes close.
I try it in the Nether?The key turns but won't take. Overworld and End only.
We fight on an End island?He never steps off a big drop. You can still be knocked off.
Two players fight him?+50% health, fixed when he wakes.
