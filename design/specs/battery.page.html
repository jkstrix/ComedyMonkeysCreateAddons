Battery & Industrial Battery

Store your spare power. Batteries fill from what your generators make beyond what's used, and give it back when they fall short. Fill a 3×3 column with them and they become one Industrial Battery for your pole network.

Design ready: 90% Mid game · Industrial: late game Look: brief next

In ten lines

  • A Battery stores spare power from your lines.
  • It gives power back when generators fall short.
  • Touching Batteries side to side share one store.
  • Stacked Batteries don't share until they form.
  • Fill a 3×3 column, 3–9 high: Industrial Battery.
  • The Industrial Battery only connects to Line Posts.
  • Cables tie to its four top insulators.
  • Charge is never lost: breaking one shares it out.
  • Lightning Rod on top: storms charge it.
  • Far away, batteries keep working on paper.

How you use it

  1. Put one on a line

    Spare power fills it instead of being lost.

  2. Ride out the gaps

    Fuel runs dry, a turbine's turned down: it carries the machines.

  3. Grow a bank

    Side by side, Batteries share one store and move more at once.

  4. Build the big one

    Fill a 3×3 column; hang brass from its insulators to your poles.

The Industrial Battery

A 3×3 footprint, 3 to 9 Batteries tall. Every block holds twice what a loose Battery does. Slide the height and the charge.

54,000,000 FE · 54 Batteries

Cables only reach it from poles, tied to its four top insulators. Its level column rises and falls with the charge; the cells on a loose Battery's front do the same.

Numbers

10,000,000 FEa Battery
2,048 FE/tin or out, per Battery in a bank
20,000,000 FEper block of an Industrial Battery
540M – 1.62B FEIndustrial, 3 to 9 high

For scale: a Lava Generator makes 1,024 FE/t; an Electric Motor at full stress uses 18,432 FE/t. All of these are in the server config.

Rules

Does

  • Connect on its sides, front and back
  • Share one store with Batteries it touches
  • Form from plain Batteries, no extra parts
  • Keep every bit of charge when it forms or comes apart
  • Re-form smaller when you break a block, if it can
  • Keep its charge as an item when broken or picked up
  • Keep working far away, on paper
  • Catch lightning with a rod on top

Doesn't

  • Connect on its top or bottom
  • Let wires or machines touch the Industrial Battery
  • Join plain Batteries beside an Industrial one
  • Make power: it only stores it
  • Explode, or load chunks
  • Charge or drain while moving on a contraption

What if…?

…the column is 10 high?The bottom 9 form; the top layer stays plain Batteries.
…one block is missing?It doesn't form until the 3×3 is full.
…I break a formed block?What's left re-forms if it can: the tallest run of 3+ layers. The broken one drops with its share.
…I break the top layer?It's one layer shorter.
…two towers stand side by side?Each is its own battery; they don't join. Link them through poles.
…the Batteries cover 3×6, or 4×4?Every full 3×3 forms; overlapping ones: one, from the lowest corner.
…which way does it face?The way the last Battery you placed faces: you pick the front as you finish it.
…lightning hits a full one?It still arcs and crackles; the extra is lost.
…its chunk unloads mid-charge?Its record carries on, and it takes the record on once loaded again.
…it's blown up?It drops with its charge.
…I tie a copper span to an insulator?That insulator then takes only copper of that colour, like a pole's.
