Monster Cage & Fish Tank

The Animal Pen's two new siblings. The Monster Cage: jar one monster, feed it Monster Chow, turn the shaft, and cull the horde for its real drops; the Netherite cage holds Withers on the power grid, and they break out if the power fails. The Fish Tank: the pens' breeding, underwater, for fish, squid and axolotls.

Monster Cage in ten lines

  • Animal Pen, but for monsters.
  • Catch it in an Iron Jar.
  • Jar into cage, Monster Chow in, shaft on.
  • Faster shaft: more monsters, more chow eaten.
  • A sword or Deployer culls half.
  • Water monsters need a Flooded cage and water.
  • Netherite cage: Withers only, needs FE too.
  • Power fails: 5-minute warning, then Withers escape daily.
  • New monsters never carry armour or weapons.
  • Every number lives in the config.

How you use it

  1. Jar a monster

    Iron Jar, any health, by hand or Deployer. A Wither needs a Netherite Jar.

  2. Cage it

    Use the jar on a cage of the right tier. Flooded for water monsters.

  3. Feed it

    Monster Chow: milled flour, bone meal and cinder flour, mixed.

  4. Turn the shaft

    A new monster joins every interval. Faster shaft, sooner.

  5. Cull

    A sword or Deployer takes half. Drops go out to your funnels.

Speed sets the pace

Each new monster always costs the same Monster Chow. A faster shaft spends it sooner and costs more stress. Pick a cage and a monster, then slide the speed.

The slider uses common Create speeds. Stress drops to zero when the cage is full or out of chow.

Containment

The Netherite cage's shaft runs the farm. Its FE holds the field, shown as a blue glowing shield around the whole block that flickers when power runs short: a flat 2,048 FE/t whenever any Wither is inside, plus up to 1,024 FE/t while it farms at full speed. Below that, the field is down.

Escaping Withers come out at the nearest open space, after the vanilla 10-second charge-up. Servers can turn escapes off; then the cage just stops.

Rules

Does

  • Grow at a steady rate, faster with speed and Monstrous I–III
  • Eat Monster Chow for every new monster
  • Give real loot at vanilla rates: Looting and Fire Aspect count
  • Let Deployers catch, release and cull
  • Turn a zombie into a drowned in a Flooded cage
  • Keep working on an airship (and fail there too)
  • Keep its horde on pistons and contraptions

Doesn't

  • Copy armour, weapons or names onto new monsters
  • Give XP to Deployers
  • Grow while unloaded, moving, or on Peaceful
  • Let a jarred Wither out anywhere but a cage
  • Let anyone but its owner break a Netherite cage
  • Let even the owner break it mid-alarm
  • Mind sunlight, rain or lightning

What if…?

…the power stays off?One Wither now, then one every game day until the cage is empty.
…nobody is near?Nothing happens until a player comes within 128 blocks. Then the klaxon starts.
…the cage is built into a wall?The Wither comes out at the nearest open space.
…the FE only covers part of it?Farming stops first. Below 2,048 FE/t the field is down.
…I break it to dodge an escape?You can't. No breaking or emptying while the field is down.
…everyone walks away mid-alarm?The countdown stops, even chunk-loaded, and picks up where it left off when someone returns.
…who hears the warning?Everyone within 128 blocks: chat at the start and each minute, a live hotbar countdown for the last 30 s.
…my airship loses power?A Wither escapes in mid-air.
…I jar a zombie in diamond armour?The copies arrive bare. Only its loot table drops.
…I drop a full Iron Jar in lava?The monster is lost. Netherite Jars can't burn or blow up.
…a Dispenser fires a jar?It releases the monster. Not a Wither: those only go into cages.
…a Flooded cage runs dry?It stops growing. The ones inside are fine.
…I put a zombie in a Flooded cage?It drowns into a drowned, and the cage makes drowned.
…I stand in front of a drowned cage?If one is armed, tridents fly out of the front every 3 s. Cull (armed go first) or stand aside.
…I want it faster?Monstrous I–III, from Librarians, on any cage, Withers included. Fruitful stays on pens and tanks.
…I upgrade a cage with monsters in it?Refused. Only empty cages and tanks upgrade.
…I use the wrong catcher?Refused, with a hint: bottles for animals and fish, Iron Jars for monsters, Netherite Jars for the Wither.
…I eat Monster Chow?Like rotten flesh. Sometimes you belch a monster's noise, or get green, nauseous Chow Breath.

Fish Tank in ten lines

  • Animal Pen, but underwater.
  • Catch it in a Capture Bottle.
  • Bottle into tank, water piped in.
  • Fish Food in, shaft on.
  • Every adult breeds every 5 minutes.
  • Each birth uses food and water.
  • Sword culls half; bucket scoops one.
  • Blue axolotls can turn up.
  • Axolotls moved here from the pens.
  • Glow squid tanks glow.
