Clockwork Jetpack

The Create Jetpack, run on a Juggernaut's Mainspring instead of air. It's worn in its own slot, so your chestplate and backpack stay on. Its brass wings unfold for powered flight.

Ready for development · 85% End game Curios: new Jetpack slot 45 decisions New enchantment: Overwound

In ten lines

  • Jetpack runs on a Mainspring, not air.
  • Wear it in the new Jetpack slot.
  • Chestplate and backpack stay on.
  • Wind it at a Jetpack Winding Station on a shaft.
  • Jump up, Sneak down, G engine, H hover.
  • Hover holds you completely still.
  • J in the air (or double-tap Jump): wings out, elytra glide; hold Jump to boost.
  • Flight is a bit faster than an elytra.
  • No wind with wings out: glide down safely.
  • Costs a Juggernaut's Mainspring; Netherite survives lava.

Overwound

A new enchantment that adds wind, so every mode lasts longer. It replaces Create's Capacity on the jetpack and also goes on the Spring Engine. Books come from Librarians and are applied at an anvil, never from the enchanting table.

How you use it

  1. Earn the springBeat a Clockwork Juggernaut. One Mainspring per kill.
  2. Build itMechanical Crafters, in Create Jetpack's pattern.
  3. Wind itAt its Winding Station on a shaft: hang it there, or back in wearing it.
  4. Wear itIn the Jetpack slot, next to your backpack and chestplate.
  5. FlyHop with the jetpack, then press J for wings and go far.

Rules

Does

  • Fly exactly like Create's Jetpack, same keys.
  • Give backtank armour, in the slot or the chest.
  • Let Create's own Jetpacks use the Jetpack slot too (with armour).
  • Keep its wind as a block, item, through death and smithing.
  • Glide safely when it runs dry with the wings out.
  • Take Overwound I–III for more wind; III doubles it.
  • Go in the chest slot when Curios isn't installed.

Doesn't

  • Hold air or feed a Diving Helmet.
  • Fly together with a chest jetpack or elytra; one at a time, Clockwork first.
  • Open its wings underwater or while riding anything.
  • Wind itself, or get placed as a block; only its Winding Station winds it.
  • Use wind in Creative.
  • Take Create's Capacity; Overwound replaces it.
  • Add any new source of Mainsprings.

What if…?

…it runs out while hovering high?You fall. At 10% left a warning sounds and the key ticks slower.
…I put a backtank or Create Jetpack in the chest?It can't be worn with this one: it pops back into your inventory.
…I dive into water in flight mode?The wings fold; jetpack mode with swim thrust takes over.
…I sit on a train seat mid-flight?Flight stops and the wings fold.
…I back into the Winding Station?Sneak with your back to it: the arm swings out and winds the key you're wearing.
…the station breaks with a jetpack on it?The jetpack drops, with its wind.
…both jetpack mods are installed?G and H toggle both; only one flies.
…there's no Curios?It goes in the chest slot and works the same.
…I fly into lava?The Netherite one keeps flying; the plain one doesn't burn as an item.
