Helper Bot

Trade Bot's cousin, bolted in place. Put him where you'd put a Blaze Burner and he does its job by Create's exact rules: he drives your train, or keeps your shop. The difference is that you can watch him do it.

Design ready: 90% Early–mid game Crafting table · andesite tier Look: brief written

In ten lines

  • Helper Bot replaces the Blaze Burner. Same rules.
  • Put him right behind the Train Controls.
  • Right-click him with a Schedule. He drives.
  • Empty hand takes the schedule back.
  • Beside a Stock Ticker, he keeps shop.
  • He never moves. He can't fall off.
  • Crafting table plus one dye recolours him.
  • Bulk washing makes him plain again.
  • Low roof? He ducks. Still works.
  • No power, no owner, no bonuses.

How you use him

  1. Craft him

    Andesite casing, electron tube, cogwheel, 2 iron sheets. No Nether trip.

  2. Dye him

    Optional. Helper Bot + 1 dye in a crafting table.

  3. Place him

    Right behind the Train Controls. He turns to face them.

  4. Hand him a Schedule

    Assemble, then right-click him. His train cap pops on.

  5. Watch him drive

    Throttle, brake, clock checks, toots and waves.

Where he goes

The same spots as a Blaze Burner. On a train: directly behind the controls, along the train, with the controls facing him. One driver per direction. In a shop: beside the Stock Ticker, on the same level.

A two-way train needs one at each end. Under a solid block he ducks into one block and keeps working.

What the train makes him do

The rules never change. Only the show does.

Speeding upPushes the throttle, leans in
CruisingRelaxes, glances out, waves at players by the track
BrakingHauls back and braces
Waiting at a stationChecks his clock, taps his wheel
Leaving a stationToot and a little wave
The train honksPulls the whistle cord
A player drivesFolds his arms; afterwards points at the schedule until you right-click him
No scheduleWaits, looks at you expectantly
No pathShrugs, looks at the map
Crash or derailJolts, goes dizzy, sulks until re-railed

One dye, one bot

Dyed as an item in a crafting table, and re-dyed the same way. Bulk washing makes him plain again; Dragons Plus Bulk Coloring works too. A placed bot can't be dyed. The colour goes on his body panels, muted to the house palette (the same colours as dyed Fluid Lines): his chest and side panels and his forearms.

Rules

Does

  • Drive trains under Create's own conductor rules
  • Keep shop beside a Stock Ticker
  • Wear a train cap while driving with a schedule
  • Wear a logistics cap in a shop
  • Turn to face the controls (in a shop he faces you)
  • Duck under low roofs
  • Show direction, schedule and status on goggles

Doesn't

  • Move, wander or fall off
  • Need FE, fuel or winding
  • Belong to anyone
  • Resume a paused schedule by himself
  • Heat basins like a Blaze Burner
  • Fly Aeronautics ships (yet)
  • Give any bonus over a Blaze Burner

What if…?

…there are two bots on one end?Only the one right behind the controls drives. The other rides along.
…I drive the train myself?The schedule pauses (Create's rule). Right-click him afterwards to resume.
…the train is disassembled?He's a block again. The schedule goes back as Create does it.
…the roof is one block above the floor?He ducks to fit and keeps working.
…a zombie walks up?He shrinks down but never leaves. Mobs ignore him.
…it rains on him?He wipes his lenses. Thunder makes him flinch.
…he's beside a ticker while driving?The train cap wins.
…the chunk unloads?Colour and facing are saved. The animation restarts.
…he's on an airship?A passenger who idles.
…I right-click him off duty?He waves.
