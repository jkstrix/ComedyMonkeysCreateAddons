Electric Lights

A light for every place: street lamps on stacking posts, wall, ceiling and hanging lamps, strips and edges, festoons, a Floodlight and more. Cheap to make, fed by your Power Lines, recoloured with a dye you keep.

Built · in the mod 23 lights Early game FE via Power Lines

In ten lines

  • Lights run on FE from Power Lines.
  • A line must touch the light.
  • Lines hidden in walls still count.
  • Strips and edges pass power along themselves.
  • Craft an assembly, stonecut your light.
  • Right-click a dye: glow changes, dye kept.
  • Breaking drops it plain again.
  • Wrench steps the brightness.
  • Light Switch or Breaker switches circuits.
  • Street lamps stack like power poles.

Try it

One street, two circuits. The dye colours the lamps' glow; the light on the ground stays white, as it does in Minecraft, unless Colorful Lighting is installed.

How you use it

  1. Craft Light Assemblies: glass, copper sheet, redstone, two at a time.
  2. Stonecut the light the place needs. Big ones are crafted.
  3. Wire it: a Power Line touching it, hidden in the wall if you like.
  4. Dress it: dye the glow, step the brightness with the wrench.
  5. Switch it: a Light Switch by hand, a Breaker by redstone.

Rules

Does

  • Light from any Power Line touching it, even one encased behind it.
  • Keep the dye: it's never used up.
  • Colour a whole strip run at once (sneak for one block).
  • Wash plain with a water bucket, which stays full.
  • Flicker for a second when power runs out, then go dark.
  • Swap style in the stonecutter, within its size group.

Doesn't

  • Take redstone itself: switch the circuit instead.
  • Colour the world's light without Colorful Lighting: then only the glow.
  • Pass power on, except along its own strip, edge or festoon.
  • Fill the recipe book with colours.
  • Drop its dye: broken lights come back plain.
  • Hurt anyone, start fires or mind the rain.

What if…?

The generator runs dry?Lights flicker for a second, then go dark.
The line is hidden in the wall?The light on it still gets power.
A block is put in a Floodlight's beam?The spot lands on that block until it's cleared.
Lights ride a moving contraption?They keep their glow but light nothing and draw nothing.
A Pendant 10 blocks down is broken?The lamp and its cable drop back.
A dyed light is picked up with Carry On?It keeps its colour.
A Breaker switches on a whole street?Lamps come on over a second, like a ripple.
The chunk unloads?Lights there draw nothing and relight on load.

Details and later

  • Hardness: lights 1, Floor Light 3, Lamp Posts 2.
  • Anyone who can use the block can dye, wash, switch or dim it.
