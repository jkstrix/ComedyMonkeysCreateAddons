Clockwork Tubes

Suck items from many places into one. Inlets take them in, tubes carry them where you can see them, and one cog-driven Receiver pushes them out through the middle of its hollow cog.

Ready for development · 85% Andesite age · Brass Inlet: brass 4 SU per RPM Look: to be designed

If you read nothing else

  • Inlets suck items in, facing any way.
  • One Receiver spits them out.
  • Tubes carry items between them; you can see them.
  • Only one Receiver per network.
  • Receiver runs on a cog; items pass through its middle.
  • Faster cog: more items, more stress.
  • Plain Inlet: one item. Brass: stacks, with a filter.
  • Redstone turns an Inlet off; a Clutch stops everything.
  • Full chest at the end: everything waits, nothing lost.
  • Max 256 tubes; no chunk loading.

How you use it

  1. Put an Inlet where items collect, facing any way: on a chest, over a depot or belt, over a floor.
  2. Run tubes from every Inlet to one spot. Branch and join as you like.
  3. Put the Receiver there, its output into a chest, vault or belt.
  4. Mesh a cogwheel with its hollow cog.
  5. Watch items slide past the windows and pop out through the cog.

Speed buys items, and costs stress

One load per turn. A plain Inlet's load is one item; a Brass Inlet's is up to a stack. Each Inlet also waits 8 ticks between loads, like a funnel.

The rules

Does

  • Take from chests, vaults, depots, belts and the floor
  • Let Inlets face any of the six directions
  • Branch in every direction
  • Insert into the block in front of the Receiver, or drop if there's none
  • Wait when the end is full: one load per tube, nothing lost
  • Work on Aeronautics ships
  • Close a tube joint with a wrench, so networks run side by side

Doesn't

  • Allow two Receivers on one network (the tube stays capped)
  • Send items backwards, or to more than one place
  • Load chunks, or move items in chunks that aren't running
  • Join Create's Chutes and Funnels, or the Fluid and Power Lines
  • Hide inside other blocks
  • Run while on a moving contraption
  • Grow past 256 tubes

What if…?

You break a tube with an item in it?The item drops there. The rest carry on, or wait for the gap to be mended.
The chest at the end fills up?Items queue back up the tubes and the Inlets stop pulling.
Two networks, each with a Receiver, touch?The tube between them stays capped. Goggles say why.
An Inlet's chunk isn't loaded?It skips its turn. Nothing is lost.
The cog stops, or the network overstresses?Nothing moves; items stay in their tubes.
An Inlet faces up?It takes items lying on top of it, like a collecting floor.
A floor Inlet and a hopper both see an item?Whichever takes it first gets it.
You die under a floor Inlet?Your drops get sucked up, as a hopper would take them.
