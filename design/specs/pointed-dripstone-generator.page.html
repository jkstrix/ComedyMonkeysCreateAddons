Pointed Dripstone Generator

A stone-generator cousin that grows Pointed Dripstone. Four in by hand, water piped in, a shaft in the back.

Ready for development · 95% Andesite + Wireless Andesite Stone generator family

If you read nothing else

  • Makes Pointed Dripstone inside a block.
  • A hammer knocks each grown tip off.
  • Put four Pointed Dripstone in by hand.
  • Pipe water into a side.
  • Turn a shaft into the back.
  • All four grow, then the hammer knocks each off.
  • One every 5 seconds at 256 RPM.
  • Uses 100 mB of water each.
  • Holds 64; full, it stops.
  • Funnels or the Wrench screen take them out.
  • No stress when stopped; the Wrench screen returns the seed.

How it works

  1. 1
    Seed itRight-click with Pointed Dripstone. It takes four and keeps them.
  2. 2
    Water itPipe water into any side but the front and back. Tank: 1,000 mB.
  3. 3
    Turn itA shaft into the back. Faster rotation grows faster.
  4. 4
    It growsAll four tips grow together, taking 400 mB (100 a piece) as they start.
  5. 5
    Knock!The hammer goes round the four, cracking each tip off: one drops into the store on every strike. Automatic: you never harvest.
  6. 6
    Take themFunnels, hoppers, chutes or arms, or by hand in the Wrench screen.
  7. ↻
    Keep it fedWater, rotation and an emptied output keep it running.

Pointed Dripstone an hour, by shaft speed. A cycle of four is 400 × 256 ÷ RPM ticks; the hammer's round of knocks is a fixed 8 s of it. All of these are config values.

Vanilla farm~60/h

A big 100-column vanilla farm makes about 60 an hour. Like the cages and tanks, it's judged against the best vanilla farm, and its pace is limited by SU and water, not by cutting what it makes.

Rules

Does

  • Keep the four seed Pointed Dripstone, and drop them when broken.
  • Take water from pipes, Fluid Lines or a Wellspring.
  • Let funnels pull its output from any side.
  • Hand out its output and seed in the Wrench screen, with quick moves.
  • Switch off with redstone, or a Redstone Link (Wireless).
  • Show how full it is on a comparator.
  • Work in every dimension, the Nether included.

Doesn't

  • Take the seed from funnels or Deployers: hand only.
  • Accept lava or any fluid but water.
  • Take water through the front or the back.
  • Put stress on the network while it's stopped.
  • Grow while riding a contraption (it keeps everything).
  • Come in a Brass tier.
  • Glow: there's no lava inside.

What if…?

…it's broken mid-growth?That growth is lost. The seed and the output drop; the water is lost.
…the water runs out mid-growth?The piece already paid for finishes. The next one waits for water.
…it has only three seeds?It waits, showing "Needs 4 Pointed Dripstone".
…someone pipes in lava?Refused. Water only.
…it's overstressed or stopped?It idles at no stress, and a half-grown piece waits.
…its chunk unloads?Nothing happens until it loads again. No catch-up.
…a contraption carries it?It keeps its seed, water, output and growth, but doesn't grow while moving.
…a Deployer offers it Pointed Dripstone?Nothing: the seed is hand only and the output takes no inserts.
