Clockwork Dynamo

The Electric Motor in reverse. Rotation goes in the front, electricity comes out of the base, and three quarters of it survives the trip.

Ready for development · 90% Brass tier 75% efficient Look: to be designed

If you read nothing else

  • The motor in reverse: rotation in, electricity out.
  • Shaft in the front, power out the base.
  • Wrench it to set how much stress it takes.
  • 75% comes out as power. Always loses some.
  • More power takes more SU, always in step.
  • Not turning or overstressed: no power.
  • The aerial up means it's working.
  • Far away, only free power (water wheels, windmills) counts.
  • Use a Clutch to turn it off.
  • Bot parts, no face, one wobbly aerial.

Turn it up, watch the cost

Drag the setting. The power it makes and the SU it takes rise together. A motor at the same setting would need more than the dynamo makes; that gap is the loss.

How you use it

  1. Build a shaft source: water wheels, a windmill, a boiler.
  2. Run the shaft into the dynamo's front.
  3. Run a Power Line or machine off its base.
  4. Wrench it and set the stress. The screen shows the power.
  5. The aerial pops up. Lights and motors on the line run.

Burning lava? Use the real generators

The dynamo is for free rotation and steam. On lava, the purpose-built generators stay ahead.

The rules

    Does

  • Take its set SU at any shaft speed
  • Make 1 FE/t for every 21⅓ SU
  • Push power out of its base every tick
  • Keep its stress even when nothing takes the power
  • Run on paper far away, on water wheels and windmills
  • Keep its setting on a contraption or in Carry On

    Doesn't

  • Make power when stopped, overstressed or moving
  • Count motors, Magma Engines or steam on paper
  • Take power in, or connect on any face but the base
  • Have redstone control (use a Clutch)
  • Keep its store or setting when broken
  • Have a face: bot parts, one aerial, nothing more

What if…?

A motor and a dynamo share a shaft?The motor needs 1,024 FE/t but gets 768. It runs down and stops.
Windmills and a Magma Engine on one shaft, far away?On paper it gives only the windmills' share.
It's set higher than the shaft can give?The network overstresses; the dynamo makes nothing.
Nothing is plugged into the base?The store fills and the rest is lost. The aerial stays up.
The shaft speed keeps changing?Same SU, same power. Only the setting matters.
Two dynamos on one shaft?Each takes its own setting.
It was overstressed when the chunk unloaded?Nothing on paper until it runs again.
A server sets efficiency to 100%?The loop breaks even and ties the Lava Generator. The config warns about this.
