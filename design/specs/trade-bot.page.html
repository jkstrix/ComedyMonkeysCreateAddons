Trade Bot

A clockwork robot who sells your stuff to seated villagers for emeralds.

Ready to build · v1 Mid-late game Runs on FE
If you read nothing else

The 10-second version

  • Sells your stuff to villagers for emeralds
  • Villagers must be sitting on seats
  • Crafted on his winding station; power it with FE
  • Link 4 inputs and 1 output at the station
  • Carries 27 stacks plus an emerald pouch
  • Trade XP arrives as Nuggets of Experience
  • Real villager trades: stock and restocks apply
  • Goes home to charge: night, storms, idle, low charge
  • No ladders or bubble columns; avoids water and lava
  • Owner only; can't be killed; a wrench packs him
  • Works unattended in chunk-loaded areas
How it works

One trip, start to finish

  1. Charges at his stationFE goes in from underneath the station, 64 FE per tick.
  2. Hops out when there's workIt's daytime, he has charge, and a seated villager wants something in his inputs.
  3. Loads upTakes what buyers want from your input containers, up to 27 stacks.
  4. TradesRolls to each seated buyer, nearest first, and trades from up to a block away.
  5. Delivers the emeraldsThey go to your output (a belt, chest, barrel or depot), and unsold items go back to the inputs.
  6. Goes homeReverse-hops into his station; his lights go out and the station glows.

↻ Repeats whenever villagers restock.

Rules

What he does and doesn't do

Does

  • Trades only with seated villagers and wandering traders
  • Uses real trades, stock and restocks
  • Earns his own reputation, so prices slowly improve
  • Puts trade XP in the output as Nuggets of Experience
  • Uses any container as an input or output
  • Opens doors, trapdoors and gates
  • Hops stairs, slabs and blocks up to one high
  • Keeps working when you're offline, if his area is loaded

Doesn't

  • Buy things (the v2 Trade Ledger adds that)
  • Drop XP orbs by the villagers
  • Climb ladders or use bubble columns
  • Go into water or lava
  • Die: at 0 HP he winds down until you wind him with a wrench
  • Get attacked by monsters, though they still scare him
  • Let anyone but his owner change his jobs
  • Ride trains, carts or seats yet
Going home

He goes back to his station when…

  • it's night
  • there's a thunderstorm
  • he's low on charge
  • there's nothing to sell
  • nobody seated is buying
  • a redstone signal pauses him
  • you hit him with a wrench
Edge cases

What if…?

  • The villager stands up mid-trade?He shrugs, keeps everything and tries again later.
  • A player is trading with that villager?The player always goes first.
  • Two bots want the same villager?First come, first served. The other finds a different buyer.
  • The output is full?He waits beside it, looking worried, and retries.
  • His chunk unloads mid-trip?Everything is saved. He picks up where he left off.
  • Only a chunk loader is keeping him loaded?He works exactly as normal.
  • A container or villager is in an unloaded chunk?He skips it this trip and tries again later.
  • You pack the station while he's out?He's called home first, then both pack into one item.
  • An input or output gets removed?Items go back where they came from, or to his station.
  • He's pushed into water?He winds down until he's out and dry (about 5 s).
  • He winds down in lava, or falls into the void?He's sent back to his station, still wound down.
  • A hostile mob turns up?He hides in a corner, shaking, until it's safe again.
  • He's running low far from home?He never leaves without enough charge to get back.
