Splitter screen, on a Power Line (preview)

The Power Line Splitter goes on a junction (a T or a cross) on a floor, wall or ceiling, and a wrench opens this. One row per branch: which way it goes, what's down it, and its priority, scrolled between First, Second and Last. When there isn't enough power, the First branches get all they want, then the Second, then the Last; branches on the same level share evenly (all on one level is plain even sharing, the default). A branch with only generators down it shows Power in instead of a field. The read-out says how it stands right now. On the block, the dial's pointer points at the First branch (no pointer when they're all level).
