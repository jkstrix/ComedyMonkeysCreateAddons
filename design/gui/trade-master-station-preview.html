Trade Master station screen

Netherite band, with the Working on row back: the gauge he's filling gets its own row, and every other gauge on his network goes in a list below that scrolls with the mouse wheel when there are more than five. His reach stays 64 blocks from the station. Example: he's out buying bookshelves at 72%, with eight gauges on his boards. An enchanted book is named by its enchantment (Mending), as that's what its gauge counts; hovering a row shows the item's tooltip.

  • Top: what he's doing (Fetching, Selling, Buying, Delivering, Putting things away; or docked, charging, paused) and his charge.
  • Network: connected, and how many board chests he can see in range; "No Trade Boards in range" if none.
  • Working on: the gauge he's filling, how full it is and what's coming; "nothing to fill" when he's home.
  • Other gauges, in this order: ones he can't fill and why (nobody sells this, out of range, can't pay); ones waiting their turn, or that another Trade Master is filling; then full ones.
  • Call home, as Trade Bot's. No container links: he works only through his network.
