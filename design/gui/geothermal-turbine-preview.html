Geothermal Turbine screen (preview)

Right-click the turbine's controller with a wrench to open it (sneak-wrench still turns or picks it up, as now). Scroll the Power field to set its level in 10% steps; it takes effect when the screen closes, like the Electric Motor's. The turbine always runs at this level while it has lava and water, and burns them in proportion. Andesite band for the turbine's trim, over its dark blue-grey steel; laid out like the motor's screen (shown below for comparison).
